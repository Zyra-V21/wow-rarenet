# RareNet

The rare hunting addon built for WoW Forever: live rare sightings shared between players and tagged by layer, plus Forever's own rares, spawn points and drops.

Download it on CurseForge: https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/rarenet

## What it does

- **Shares every rare, live.** When a RareNet player sees a rare (nameplates, target or mouseover), everyone else gets it within seconds: name, zone, coordinates, alive or dead, and layer.
- **Knows your layer.** It reads the creatures around you to tell which layer you are on, so a sighting shows as on your layer or on another one.
- **Made for Forever.** The rares that are new in Forever, their spawn points and their new drops, and the rares nobody has located yet, to be found first.
- **Rare database** (`/rarenet db`): 3D model, level, spawn points, live status, drops learned by the community, and how to fight each rare.
- **Map and minimap pins** for known spawns and live sightings.
- **Checks itself.** Sightings expire, a report is confirmed only when two players saw it, and false reports get refuted by players who walk past.

RareNet only shares what players see: it never moves your character, never targets anything and never changes your layer on its own. For a rare on another layer, a right click can ask Layer-Swap or Forever Layer Hop to take you there. It stays quiet in dungeons, raids and battlegrounds.

## Install

Get it from CurseForge (website or app), or copy the `RareNet` folder into your game's `Interface/AddOns` folder.

## Development

- `npm install`, then `npm test`: runs `tests/test_rarenet.lua` in a Lua VM (fengari) against a small mock of the game API (`tests/wow.lua`).
- `sh tools/package.sh <dir>`: builds the release zip (`RareNet-<version>.zip`) into `<dir>`.
- `RareNet/Seed.lua`, `ForeverRares.lua`, `ForeverLoot.lua`, `Loot.lua` and `Tactics.lua` are generated by the scripts in `tools/` from the source data listed below, which is not part of this repository. `data/` holds the hand-curated rare data and tips (see `data/README.md`).
